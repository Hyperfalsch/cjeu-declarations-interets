Timeline of declarations of interests

This page tracks successive versions of the declarations of interests made public by members of the Court of Justice of the European Union and the General Court of the European Union.

For each person, the timeline lists the versions identified, a link to the official source, and, where possible, an archived copy.

Note: this website is not affiliated with the Court of Justice of the European Union. It is an independent documentary tracking tool.
