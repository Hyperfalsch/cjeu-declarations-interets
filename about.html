Why this website?

This website aims to make it easier to follow the declarations of interests published by members of the Court of Justice of the European Union and the General Court of the European Union.

Its purpose is to keep a simple record of successive versions of these declarations, with links to official sources and, where possible, archived copies.

Who am I?

My name is Pascal Hansens. I am a journalist, member of Investigate Europe, interested in EU affairs and transparency.

This project is an independent documentary tracking tool. It is not affiliated with the Court of Justice of the European Union.
